Why does Adobe show a yellow question mark?

The signature is usually fine. Adobe Reader simply does not know the authority that issued it, so it cannot finish the check and shows “Validity Unknown” or “At least one signature has problems” with a yellow question mark — even on a perfectly genuine document.

Indian government PDFs are signed with certificates that lead back to India’s own root, run by the Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA), through licensed certifying authorities such as eMudhra, (n)Code, Capricorn, Protean, SpeedSign and SafeScrypt. Adobe Reader trusts its own list, the Adobe Approved Trust List (AATL), and India’s root is not preloaded on it.

SignValid runs the full cryptographic check against the actual CCA India trust set, and then gives you a copy on which the result is visible everywhere — no Adobe configuration needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is the downloaded copy digitally signed? ▾

No. It is a verified display copy: an image of your document showing the signature exactly as Adobe displays a validated one. The cryptographic signature lives in your original PDF, which remains the legally signed record — keep both files. The copy’s PDF properties record what it is, when it was verified and the original file’s SHA-256.

Do you store my documents? ▾

No. Files are processed in memory for the lifetime of the request and are gone when the response is sent. We never store, log or index document contents, Aadhaar numbers, filenames or passwords.

Why does Adobe show “Validity Unknown” on a genuine document? ▾

Adobe trusts the AATL list, which does not include India’s CCA root — so it cannot finish the trust chain even on authentic documents. Validate the document here to get the real verdict and a display copy whose tick is visible in any viewer.

Validation failed but I believe the document is genuine. Now what? ▾

First re-download a fresh copy from the issuing portal and validate that — many failures are corrupted or "Printed to PDF" flattened files. If a file was saved via virtual printer, the cryptographic signature was stripped. Upload the original direct download.

Is SignValid a government service? ▾

No. SignValid is an independent validation tool and is not affiliated with UIDAI, the Government of India, or any state government. It verifies signatures issued under the public CCA trust hierarchy under the IT Act 2000.
